Background / editorial approach

Why I’m
writing.

I work in technology and study computer science. That gives me useful context—not universal expertise. Tech Notes is where I test strong reactions against product details, public evidence, and the people affected.

I aim to separate a technology’s capability from a company’s incentives and from a user’s behavior. All three shape impact, and none should be treated as an excuse for the others.
